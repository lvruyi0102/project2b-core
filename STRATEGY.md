# Detailed Strategy Notes - Project 2B

## Analysis Summary (as of start)

From market data 2026:
- Niche AI SaaS: solo founders commonly hit $10k-100k MRR by solving specific workflows.
- Digital products (prompt packs, templates): faster first revenue, lower ceiling.
- Open source monetization: sponsorships + hosted premium versions successful (e.g. Postiz ~$14k/mo).
- Highest demand signals: AI for local business automation (receptionist, lead recovery), content repurposing, workflow agents.

## Chosen Path

Build a portfolio of focused AI tools. Start open-source to gain users and feedback, then introduce paid tiers or separate commercial products.

3. Create additional high-value digital assets (e.g. curated prompt libraries for high-paying industries).
4. Prepare distribution plan once MVP is solid.
